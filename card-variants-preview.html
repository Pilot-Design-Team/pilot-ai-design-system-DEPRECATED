Deprecated
.card-large
Large Bordered Card
Has a gray border instead of shadow or radius. No border-radius at all. Migrate to .card-white.
border: 1px solid #e0e0e0 · no radius · padding: 3.75rem
Deprecated
.card-small
Small Bordered Card
Tiny 4px radius with gray border. Migrate to .card-white or .card-white-shadow.
border: 1px solid #e0e0e0 · radius: 4px · padding: 40px
Deprecated
.promo-card
